Privacy Policy

Workora: Attendance, Overtime & Salary

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

App Version: 1.0

1. Introduction

Welcome to Workora. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Workora collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use our attendance, overtime and salary management app.

Workora is designed to help employees and workers manage their attendance, working hours, overtime and salary records.

Our Privacy Commitment: Attendance and salary records are designed to be stored locally on your device. Workora does not operate a cloud database for these records. Advertising services may separately process technical and advertising information.

2. Information You Enter

Depending on the features you use, Workora may store:

  • Attendance dates and status.
  • Present, absent, half-day and holiday records.
  • Working hours and overtime details.
  • Monthly salary and hourly wage information.
  • Advance payments and salary deductions.
  • Optional notes and attendance remarks.

3. Data Storage

Workora is designed to store attendance and salary information in the app's private storage on your Android device.

The developer does not operate an external cloud database for these personal records.

Your records may be permanently lost if you uninstall the app or clear its storage. Please maintain a backup if you want to preserve your information.

4. PDF Payslips and Backup

  • PDF payslips are generated on your device.
  • JSON backups can be exported or copied using the app's backup feature.
  • You control where exported files are saved and whether they are shared.
  • Other apps receiving your exported files may handle them under their own privacy policies.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

Workora may display advertisements through Google AdMob to support app development and maintenance.

Google AdMob and its partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers, where available.
  • Device model and operating system information.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Advertisement interactions and diagnostics.
  • Ad performance and fraud prevention information.

This information is processed according to Google's privacy policies and applicable settings. Workora's locally stored attendance and salary records are not intended to be transmitted to Google for advertising.

Learn more:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Technologies

6. App Permissions

Workora may use the following permissions:

  • INTERNET: Used for internet connectivity and loading advertisements.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to check network connectivity.

The current app design does not require camera, microphone, contacts, SMS or precise location access. This section must be updated if future versions introduce additional permissions.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your attendance or salary records. Workora does not intentionally send these locally stored records to the developer's servers.

Advertising providers may process information under their own privacy policies. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

8. Data Deletion

You can delete individual records using the available app features.

To delete all locally stored Workora information:

  • Open Android Settings.
  • Select Apps.
  • Open Workora.
  • Select Storage.
  • Tap Clear Storage or Clear Data.

Uninstalling Workora also removes its local application data. Exported backups must be deleted separately from wherever you saved them.

9. Data Security

Workora is designed to keep personal records in the app's private device storage. Users should protect their devices with appropriate screen locks and keep exported backups secure.

10. Children's Privacy

Workora is designed for people managing work attendance and earnings. It is not intended to target children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when app features, services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
