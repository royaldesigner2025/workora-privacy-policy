Privacy Policy

Workora: Attendance, Overtime & Salary

Package: com.aistudio.workora.kxmpzq Version 1.0 Effective Date: October 30, 2026

1. Executive Summary & Core Philosophy

Workora ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as an offline-first attendance, overtime tracking, and salary calculation utility for workers, freelancers, and salaried employees.

Our core architecture is built around user privacy: your attendance logs and salary records are strictly stored on your own physical device. We do not operate external database servers, we do not require account sign-ups, and we do not harvest, track, or sell your employment or financial data.

🛡️ Zero Cloud Storage of Personal Logs: All attendance statuses, wage values, and salary slips remain in your device's private sandboxed database (Android Room SQLite). If you uninstall the app or clear its data, all personal records are immediately wiped from the device.

3. Attendance Data Practices

When you mark your attendance inside Workora:

  • Input Data: Workora saves the selected date, attendance status (Present, Absent, Half Day, Shift, or Holiday), recorded shift timing, approved overtime hours, and any optional text remarks you enter.
  • Purpose: This data is solely used by the on-device algorithms to calculate your monthly attendance percentage, completed work days, and overtime totals.
  • Access Restrictions: No third party, including the app developer, has access to your attendance logs or shift schedules.

4. Salary & Earnings Data Practices

Workora features a transparent salary estimation calculator:

  • User-Configured Rates: You may enter your base monthly salary, working days per month, and overtime hourly rate.
  • Advance Loan Deductions: You can optionally record advance draws or loan deductions against a specific month.
  • Payslip Generation: Monthly PDF salary slips are compiled on-device utilizing Android's native PdfDocument graphics engine. They are saved in your application cache and are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • No Financial Account Linking: Workora does not request, link to, or access your bank accounts, credit cards, UPI, or payment gateways.

5. Advertising Identifiers (Google AdMob)

Workora displays banner advertisements supplied by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to support the ongoing development, updates, and maintenance of the free application.

What AdMob & Google Play Services Collect:

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically process certain technical and pseudonymous information from your device, including:

  • Google Advertising ID (AAID): A unique, user-resettable identifier provided by Google Play Services used for advertising attribution, frequency capping (preventing repetitive ads), and fraud detection.
  • Device Telemetry: Device manufacturer, hardware model, operating system version, screen resolution, mobile carrier, and interface language.
  • Approximate Location: Derived from your coarse IP address (country or city level) to ensure region-appropriate advertisements.
  • Diagnostic Metrics: Ad request latency, loading success/failure errors, and user interactions with banner ads.

⚙️ How to Opt-Out or Reset Your Advertising ID:
You can control or delete your Advertising ID at any time via Android device settings:
Open Android Settings > Google > Ads > Tap "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
You can also toggle off "Personalized Ads" to only receive contextual, non-targeted advertisements.

6. Device Permissions Used

Workora utilizes the principle of least privilege and requests only standard install-time permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required solely by Google AdMob SDK to fetch ad banners over the network.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the AdMob SDK to verify whether internet connectivity is active before dispatching ad requests.

Workora does NOT request runtime permissions such as Camera, Microphone, Precise GPS Location (Fine Location), Contacts, SMS, Phone State, or External Storage.

7. Data Retention, Backup & User Rights

  • Full User Control: You can edit or delete any attendance day or advance deduction record directly from within the app at any time.
  • JSON Backup & Restore: Workora provides an export tool that copies your complete attendance database and salary configuration into a structured JSON string on your clipboard. You decide where to save or transfer this backup.
  • Permanent Data Erasure: To completely erase all Workora records, you can select "Clear Data / Clear Storage" under Android Settings > Apps > Workora, or simply uninstall the application. All local SQLite databases will be permanently destroyed.

8. Children's Privacy

Workora is designed for workforce personnel, employees, and adults managing their job attendance and earnings. We do not intentionally target, collect, or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area).
